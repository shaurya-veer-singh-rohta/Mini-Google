How MiniGoogle Works

OLD WAY – Keyword Frequency

Before Google, early search engines simply counted how many times your search term appeared on a webpage. More occurrences meant a higher ranking.

Flaw: Webmasters could easily cheat by stuffing invisible keywords (e.g., repeating "pizza" 100 times).

NEW WAY – PageRank

Google introduced PageRank. Instead of just reading text, it looks at the links connecting websites. A link from Page A to Page B is counted as a "vote" of importance.

Benefit: It simulates a random surfer clicking links. Pages receiving links from other important pages get a higher score, yielding much better results.
